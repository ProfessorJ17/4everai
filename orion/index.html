MAINFRAME : ORION Edition

This is the ORION user interface, designed for seamless interaction with various AI models and tools. Explore its unique features:

  • Advanced Theming: Choose from multiple dynamic color themes that alter the entire UI's aesthetic.
  • Agent Mode: Select specialized AI agents from various teams (e.g., Website Design, Game Design) to handle specific tasks with expert-level prompts.
  • Extensive Model Library: Access a vast collection of AI models from OpenRouter, conveniently sorted into Free and Premium tiers, including Vision-capable models.
  • Custom & Community Personas: Create and save your own AI personalities or browse and import personas shared by the community.
  • Intelligent Suggestions: Toggle the 'Q' button to get AI-generated follow-up questions based on the conversation, helping you explore topics more deeply.
  • Firebase-Powered: Full user authentication, subscription status tracking, and a credit system for free-tier users.
  • Image Support: Attach images to your prompts when using Vision-enabled AI models.
  • Conversation Management: All your chats are automatically saved and can be reloaded or deleted from the sidebar.
